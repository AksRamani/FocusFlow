Privacy Policy for FocusFlow

Last updated: October 29, 2026

FocusFlow ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a minimalist to-do and Kanban board Chrome extension designed to replace your New Tab page.

1. Data Collection and Usage

FocusFlow does NOT collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or user activity. All tasks, priorities, notes, and preferences entered into the extension are stored strictly on your local device.

2. Storage and Permissions

The extension requests the storage permission solely to save your to-do tasks, column statuses, and priority settings locally in your browser using the Chrome Storage API (chrome.storage.local).

  • No data leaves your computer.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising networks are used.
  • No account registration or external server communication is required.

3. Third-Party Access

We do not communicate with external APIs, analytics providers, or cloud servers. None of your data is shared with third parties under any circumstances.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, revisions will be published along with updated release notes.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the extension's project repository.
